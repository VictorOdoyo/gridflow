# Branch primitive and Y-bus

For `y=1/(r+jx)`, `t=tap exp(j shift)` and total charging b, the four stamps are `Yff=(y+jb/2)/|t|²`, `Yft=-y/conj(t)`, `Ytf=-y/t`, `Ytt=y+jb/2`. Positive phase shift is a from-side ideal-transformer angle. Bus shunts add `(gs+jbs)/base_mva` to the diagonal. Parallel circuits sum naturally. Negative branch reactance is allowed, but DC and fast-decoupled methods may be inappropriate. AC branches require nonzero complex impedance.
